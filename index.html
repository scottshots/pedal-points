    ❓ How to use Pedal Points

    The short version: tap Start Errand or Start Leisure when you ride, keep the app open, tap End ride when you're done, and spend your points on rewards.

    🚲 Ride tab, step by step

    1. Before your first ride: tap ⚙ at the top right. Enter your name, pick your main bike, and enter your car's miles per gallon and your local gas price. Tap Save settings / Done at the bottom.

    2. Start a ride: tap Start Errand if this ride replaces a car trip, like going to the store or the post office. Errand miles earn double points. Tap Start Leisure for a fun ride.

    3. Allow location: the first time, your phone asks to share your location. Tap Allow. The app needs it to count your miles.

    4. While you ride: keep the app open on your screen. A handlebar phone mount works best. If you switch to another app or the screen turns off, miles may stop counting.

    5. Bonus buttons: night, freezing cold, heat, snow and rain are worked out automatically from the time and the weather. If the weather is wrong, tap Snowing? or Raining? to fix it. Tap Hauling? if you're carrying a heavy load. A gold button means it's on.

    6. Riding your other bike? Tick the box under the bonus buttons before you start. Classic bike miles earn 1.5x.

    7. End the ride: tap End ride. You'll see your points, bonuses, any badges and quests you finished, and the gas money you saved.

    8. Forgot to track? Tap "Forgot to track a ride? Add it here," type the miles, and tick what applied. It earns the same points.

    🏆 Rewards tab

    Points bank: every point you earn goes here.

    Weekly quests: four small goals that reset every Monday. Finish one for bonus points.

    Prize menu: the gold bar shows how close you are. When you have enough points, tap Cash in, and the points come out of your bank.

    Badges: locked badges are grey and show what to do to earn them.

    Please note: Pedal Points does not pay out real money or gift cards. Points and reward dollars are just a game. Cashing in is your own permission to treat yourself, paid for with the money you're saving on gas by riding instead of driving.

    📊 Stats tab and levels

    Stats shows your total, errand and leisure miles, the real gas money you've saved, your streak and your points.

    Every point you ever earn counts toward your level, from Trailhead Rookie up to Summit Legend. Spending points never lowers your level.

    ⚙ Settings

    Tap ⚙ at the top right to change your name, main bike, car mileage, gas price and reward boost, mile announcements and sounds, bonus buttons and prizes, and to save backups. Tap Save settings / Done at the bottom when you're finished.

    💬 Common questions

    Tap a question to see the answer.
    Why didn't my miles count?

    Make sure you tapped Allow for location, kept the app open with the screen on, and had a clear view of the sky. Very short rides, under about a tenth of a mile, don't count. If a ride didn't record, add it with "Forgot to track a ride?"

    It says there's a location problem.

    Open Chrome, tap the three dots, then Settings, Site settings, Location, and make sure this site is allowed. Also check that location is turned on in your phone's settings.

    How are points worked out?

    Each mile earns 10 points, and errand miles count double. Bonuses are added per mile: night +10, freezing +5, heat +3, snow +15, rain +5, plus any bonus buttons you turn on. From December through March everything earns 1.5x, and classic bike miles earn 1.5x too. You also get +5 points for every 100 feet you climb, plus a streak bonus on the first ride of each day in a row.

    What's the difference between Errand and Leisure?

    Errand rides replace a trip you'd otherwise drive, so they save gas and earn double points. Leisure rides are for fun, like a ride around the lake.

    What's a classic bike?

    A regular bike with no motor. Set your main bike in Settings, and switch for a single ride on the Ride tab.

    How do I add, change or delete a prize?

    Tap ⚙ and scroll to Edit prizes. Change a price in its box, tap Delete to remove a prize, or type a name and price at the bottom and tap Add prize. Prizes sort themselves from cheapest to most expensive.

    How do I add or reorder bonus buttons?

    Tap ⚙ and scroll to Bonus buttons. Use ▲ and ▼ to change the order, Delete to remove one, or type a name and bonus points at the bottom and tap Add button. If you delete one by mistake, tap "Reset to the starting buttons."

    How do I stop the mile announcements?

    Tap ⚙, then under Sounds and announcements choose every mile, every 5, 10 or 20 miles, or Off. You can also choose a buzz only, with no voice.

    How do levels and quests work?

    Levels go up with every point you earn and never go down. Weekly quests reset every Monday, and each one you finish adds bonus points to your bank.

    How do I move to a new phone?

    Tap ⚙, then Save backup file. Send that file to your new phone, by email for example. On the new phone, open the app, tap ⚙, then Restore from file and choose it. The app reminds you to back up every 5 rides.

    Will an app update erase my points?

    No. Your points, prizes and settings stay on your phone when the app updates. Clearing Chrome's site data would erase them, so keep a backup.

    Is my location shared or tracked?

    There are no accounts and no tracking. Your rides and points stay on your phone. When you start a ride, the app sends your approximate location to a free weather service (Open-Meteo) to get the temperature, weather, sunrise and sunset. Nothing else leaves your phone.
