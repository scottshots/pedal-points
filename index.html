Meet Pedal Points in one minute

How it works

1

Start a ride

Tap Start errand ride when your bike replaces a car trip, or Start leisure ride for fun. Your phone's GPS counts the miles, and a live map pops up right under the buttons.
2

Earn points

Every mile earns points. Errand miles count double. Extra credit for hills, cold, rain, hauling a load, and riding a classic bike.
3

Treat yourself

Set your own rewards, like a coffee, an ice cream or a dinner out, and cash in your points when you've earned them.

See it in action

Built around real savings

Pedal Points ties rewards to the gas money you save. You enter your car's miles per gallon and your local gas price, and every errand mile adds up the fuel you didn't buy.

A reward boost lets you decide how much extra credit to give yourself. At 1, a plain errand mile earns exactly the gas it saved. The gas math leaves out wear and tear on your car, oil changes, your time and your mental health, so you can raise it to whatever feels fair.

Gas savings are estimates for fun, not for taxes or business mileage. Points aren't real money.

Packed with game

Levels and themes

Level up from Kickstand Rookie to Pedal Points Immortal, and unlock 10 scenery themes along the way, from Fat Bike and BMX to Night Ride and Desert Red Rock. Even the road trip stops change to match.

A road trip

Your miles move you along a 1,000-mile road trip. Finish it for a gold star, then start another lap.

Trophies and quests

Fill your trophy case and take on four new weekly quests every Monday.

Streaks

Build a riding streak, plus a daily check-in streak with a ride tip or fun fact.

Private by design

No accounts and no tracking. Your rides and points stay on your phone.

Any bike

Made for e-bikes and classic bikes alike. Classic bike miles earn a bonus: 1.5x to start, and you can set it yourself.

Max speed and conditions

See your top speed after every ride, with GPS glitches filtered out. Share cards show the weather you rode in, and max speed only if you turn it on.

Fix any ride

Forgot to end a ride and hopped in a car? Edit the distance, switch errand or leisure, or delete it, and your points adjust to match.

Help us test the Android app

The Pedal Points Android app is in testing on Google Play. Before it can go public, Google needs at least 12 testers to keep it on their phones for 14 days. It's free, there's no account, and you don't have to ride a lot. Any bike counts.

  • 1. Email us the Gmail address you use for Google Play on your Android phone.
  • 2. We'll add you to the tester list, usually within a day, and let you know.
  • 3. Open the tester link on your phone, tap Become a tester, then install Pedal Points from Google Play.
  • 4. Keep it installed for 14 days and open it now and then. Tell us what you think at hello@pedalpoints.app.
