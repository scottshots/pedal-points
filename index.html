    How to use Pedal Points

    1. First time: tap ⚙ (top right) and enter your car's mpg and your local gas price, then tap Save settings. This is how the app works out the gas money you save.

    2. Start a ride: tap Start Errand if the ride replaces a car trip (grocery store, post office), or Start Leisure for a fun ride. Allow location when asked. Keep the app open and the screen on while you ride.

    3. Bonuses: night, freezing temperatures, snow and rain are detected automatically. If the weather is wrong, tap Snowing? or Raining? to fix it. Tap Hauling? if you're carrying a heavy load. Tick the analog bike box if you're riding without a motor.

    4. End the ride: tap End ride. Your points go into your bank, including climbing and streak bonuses.

    5. Spend points: in the Prize menu, tap Cash in when you've earned enough for a treat. The gold bar shows how close you are.

    6. Change prizes: tap ⚙, then use Edit prizes. Change a price, tap Delete to remove one, or type a name and price and tap Add prize to make your own.

    7. Badges: locked badges show a hint for how to earn them.

    8. Back up: tap ⚙, then Save backup file. Use Restore from file on a new phone. The app reminds you every 5 rides.
