Privacy policy and terms of use

Effective November 7, 2026

The short version

  • Pedal Points has no accounts and doesn't track you.
  • Your rides, points and settings are stored only on your own phone. Your route is never saved.
  • To show weather and a map during a ride, the app sends your approximate location to two free outside services. No other personal information leaves your phone.
  • We don't sell, share or collect your personal information.

Who we are

Pedal Points ("the app") is a free bike-riding game made by Pedal Points. You can reach us at hello@pedalpoints.app.

What the app stores, and where

The app saves your ride totals, your last 30 rides (date, distance, points, gas saved, top speed and weather conditions), points, trophies, prizes, settings, optional first name, car mileage and gas price in the app's storage on your own device (your web browser's storage, if you use Pedal Points in a browser). While a ride is running or paused, its miles and points are saved there too, so the ride isn't lost if the app closes. This information is not sent to us or anyone else. Clearing your browser's site data, or uninstalling the app, deletes it. You can save and restore a backup file yourself from the app's Settings. In the Android app, a backup file is also saved automatically after every ride to the Documents › Pedal Points folder on your phone. It stays on your phone unless you choose to send a copy somewhere, such as your email.

Location

The app uses your phone's location only while a ride is running, to count miles and feet climbed. Your route is kept only in the app's memory on your phone, so you can choose to share its shape after the ride. It is never saved, uploaded or stored anywhere else.

In the Android app, location keeps working during a ride even when your screen is off or you switch to another app, so your miles keep counting. While it does, a notification shows that a ride is in progress, and the app asks your permission to show that notification. Location stops as soon as you pause or end the ride. The app doesn't send any other notifications.

When a ride starts, the app sends your approximate location to these services:

  • Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com), to get the temperature, weather, sunrise and sunset.
  • OpenStreetMap (openstreetmap.org), to load map pictures of your area, if the live map is turned on. You can turn the map off in Settings.

Like any website, these services receive your device's internet address when they respond. Their own privacy policies apply to that information.

Other outside services

  • Google Fonts provides the app's lettering, and cdnjs provides the map code. They receive a normal web request from your device.
  • Sharing: when you tap Share, the app makes a picture on your phone and opens your phone's own share menu. Nothing is posted unless you choose an app and post it yourself. Your route shape is left off the picture unless you choose to include it, and it's only offered right after a ride.
  • Photos: if you add a photo to a share card, the app uses it only to draw that card on your phone. The photo isn't uploaded or saved by the app, and redrawing it removes the hidden location data that phone photos often carry.
  • Tips: the "Buy the maker a coffee" button opens PayPal. Any tip is handled by PayPal under its own terms, and the app never sees your payment details.

Children

Pedal Points is made for adults. Anyone under 18 should use it only with a parent's or guardian's permission. We don't knowingly collect information from children, and the app doesn't collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
